On-device summary. This page is a short summary shown locally on your camera. It is not the full policy. The complete, authoritative privacy policy is published by QwickApps and linked below.

What the device processes

Your camera processes video and audio streams according to the Zone & AI Rules you configure on the device itself. See the device's own Zone & AI Rules settings page in this WebUI for the rules currently in effect. Processing is local by default and under your control.

Outdoor zones

Outdoor zones are logging-only by default — they record local event logs rather than continuously streaming or uploading media. Any change to that behaviour is something you configure yourself.

Optional Momo voice feature

The Momo voice feature is optional. When enabled it listens only for the wake word you configure; it does not continuously record or transmit audio. Disabling the feature turns it off entirely.

Nothing leaves your local network by default

No data is sent off your local network unless you opt into QwickWatch Service cloud features. You choose which features to enable, and you can turn them off again at any time in the WebUI.
