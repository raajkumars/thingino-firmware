What this firmware is

QwickWatch Edge OS is firmware distributed by QwickApps, based on Thingino, and runs on camera hardware that you own and control. Installing or using it does not transfer ownership of your device to QwickApps.

Local-first operation

By design this firmware is local-first: video and audio processing happens on your device, under the rules you configure in its WebUI. If you explicitly opt in, processing may also take place under your own QwickWatch Service account for the cloud features you enable. Nothing is sent to QwickApps servers without that opt-in.
